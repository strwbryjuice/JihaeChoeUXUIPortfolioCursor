Digital Product Design

3 month

2025

Problem
There is increasing numbers of study and working abroad experiences, which leads to loneliness from inevitable migration.
Goal
Allow user to have constant communication with pre-existing relationships but not interfere with their personal schedule.
Solution
Reflecting back what good digital communication is, provide an environment that keeps the balance between work and relationships.
